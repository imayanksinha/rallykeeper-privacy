RallyKeeper Privacy Policy

Developer: MSCORE Labs
App: RallyKeeper (com.mscorelabs.rallykeeper)
Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

RallyKeeper does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline. The only exception is an optional community sign-up form you can choose to open (see below).

Data you enter

You may type player names and score points. This information, together with your saved match history and statistics, is stored only on your own device. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

Permissions, internet, ads and tracking

RallyKeeper contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. Scoring, match history and statistics work fully offline. The app declares the standard Android internet-access permission only because Google Play Billing, which handles the optional one-time "Pro" purchase, requires it. RallyKeeper itself does not send your data to any server.

Sharing

If you tap "Share" on a match, the app hands a short text summary of that match, or with Pro a picture card or a CSV file of your history, to the sharing app you choose (for example a messaging app). Nothing is sent unless you do this yourself.

In-app purchase

The optional "Pro Lifetime" unlock is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment and shows you its own receipts. MSCORE Labs does not receive your card, bank or other payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Optional community sign-up

The app has an optional button that opens a Google Form in your web browser. You decide whether to enter an email address there. If you do, MSCORE Labs uses it only to send you RallyKeeper news and updates, never sells or shares it, and deletes it if you ask by emailing imayanksinha@gmail.com. The form is for people aged 18 or over. The app itself does not send or store anything when you open the form.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved match inside the app. Uninstalling RallyKeeper removes all of its data from your device.

Children

RallyKeeper does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

Contact

Questions? Email MSCORE Labs at imayanksinha@gmail.com.
